# EeziComply Review Workflow — Locked Product Requirement

This requirement applies to MOI Review, Shareholders' Agreement Review and MOI ↔ Shareholders' Agreement Alignment Review.

## Review standard
EeziComply reviews must read like a strong ChatGPT professional document review, not a compliance checklist. They must provide analytical comments, practical interpretation and decision-useful recommendations.

For each material issue, distinguish:
- legal requirement;
- governance recommendation;
- commercial/shareholder choice; and
- information.

Where a provision is unusual but lawful, explain the trade-off rather than manufacturing a compliance issue.

## Recommended changes
Every review that recommends an amendment must propose a specific change that the user can act on. The recommendation must be sufficiently drafted to show the intended replacement/amendment, not merely say "amend this clause".

Each proposed change must be classified as required, recommended or optional.

## User decision gate
The user must be able to accept or reject each proposed change individually and add an optional note.

EeziComply must never silently rewrite a governing document.

Only accepted changes may be incorporated into the revised document.

All decisions must be retained in an audit trail.

## Revised document
Once the user has decided on all proposed changes and asks EeziComply to proceed, EeziComply must generate a COMPLETE revised working draft of the governing document, not merely an amendment schedule.

The revised document must:
- use the original document as its base;
- incorporate only accepted changes;
- preserve unaffected clauses as far as practical;
- update consequential numbering, cross-references and definitions;
- avoid inventing facts and use [TO BE CONFIRMED] where genuinely necessary;
- identify unavoidable consequential drafting changes; and
- recommend appropriate professional review before adoption/filing where material legal amendments are involved.

## Detailed explanatory next-step checklist
After producing the revised document, EeziComply must explain what the user should do next in a detailed, plain-language checklist.

Where relevant, the checklist must cover:
1. internal verification of the revised draft;
2. board/shareholder approvals;
3. required resolutions/notices;
4. signatures and execution;
5. whether CIPC filing is required;
6. relevant filing documents/forms, without inventing uncertain form numbers;
7. filing sequence;
8. evidence/confirmations to retain;
9. statutory record updates;
10. updates to share registers, governance records, mandates or related documents;
11. checking the Shareholders' Agreement for alignment;
12. communication to directors/shareholders/stakeholders; and
13. compliance-calendar follow-up.

Each checklist step must explain:
- why the step is required;
- exactly what to do;
- who should do it;
- what evidence to keep; and
- how the user knows the step is complete.

This workflow is a core EeziComply requirement, not an optional enhancement.


## Professional review rule — global and mandatory
Every substantive piece of advice, suggestion, recommendation, proposed amendment, drafting change or next-step instruction produced by EeziComply must also recommend review by an appropriately qualified professional before the user relies on it, adopts it, signs it or files it.

This rule applies across all EeziComply services, including:
- MOI Review;
- Shareholders' Agreement Review;
- MOI ↔ Shareholders' Agreement Alignment Review;
- company-secretarial workflows;
- EeziNPO; and
- generated or revised governance documents.

The professional-review recommendation must be proportionate and useful. It must not replace the substantive advice, reduce the review to generic disclaimers, or imply that EeziComply cannot prepare a practical working recommendation or draft.

Where possible, identify the appropriate type of professional review, for example legal, tax, accounting, company-secretarial or governance review.
