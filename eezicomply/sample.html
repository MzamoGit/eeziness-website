7. Step-by-step filing guide

  1. Log into CIPC New e-Services yourself. EeziComply never asks for your password or OTP.
  2. Start the Beneficial Ownership filing for the correct company.
  3. Verify enterprise and filer details.
  4. Upload the signed EeziComply mandate.
  5. Capture ownership layers and natural persons using the personalised answer sheet.
  6. Upload the generated register/disclosure documents and required external evidence.
  7. Review the CIPC summary against the EeziComply pack before submission.
  8. Submit and complete the OTP yourself.
  9. Save the CIPC confirmation/certificate and tracking reference.
  10. Return to EeziComply and store the confirmation in the Vault.
