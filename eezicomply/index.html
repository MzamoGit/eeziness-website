EeziComply is the umbrella enabler for the company-secretarial, governance and compliance work a growing South African business needs to keep under control. Choose the task you need help with; EeziComply guides the process, prepares the right records and tells you what to do next.

Beneficial Ownership is one EeziComply enabler — not EeziComply itself. The same platform is being extended across governance documents, company records, statutory actions and specialist nonprofit compliance.
HOW EEZICOMPLY WORKS

Start with what happened.

Tell EeziComply what your company needs to do. The workflow then follows the legal and governance logic.

1Understand the company event or obligation
2Determine approvals, information and evidence required
3Prepare documents, registers and next-step guidance
4Keep the company record organised for future reuse
