THREE LAUNCH FUNDING PATHWAYS

Funding is bigger than purchase orders.

EeziFund started with purchase-order finance, but SMEs need a broader funding route. The platform now supports three initial funding pathways while the funder and mandate universe continues to expand.

PO

PO & Contract Finance

Funding linked to a credible purchase order, awarded contract or defined transaction.

  • Transaction economics
  • Buyer / issuer assessment inputs
  • Supplier and delivery information
WC

Working Capital

Funding to support the operating cycle where cash is tied up in customers, stock or day-to-day growth.

  • Bank-statement evidence
  • Management accounts
  • Debtors / creditors information
TG

SME Term & Growth Finance

Term funding for expansion, equipment-light growth initiatives or other defined business funding needs.

  • Funding purpose and term
  • Trading and turnover profile
  • Profitability and existing debt
HOW EEZIFUND WORKS

Prepare once. Improve the application. Match intelligently.

The aim is not to send the same application everywhere. EeziFund uses structured information and funding criteria to identify where there may be a fit and what needs to improve where there is not.

1Business profileCapture the core company and trading information once.
2Funding needChoose PO / contract, working capital or term / growth finance.
3DocumentsUpload relevant evidence and reuse current documents on later applications.
4Readiness & matchingSee gaps, strengths and mandate-level funding potential.
5Controlled routingEligible applications can be referred only to participating, activated funding partners.
WHAT MAKES THE MODEL DIFFERENT

Useful even before a funder says yes.

A funding application should tell the SME more than whether one lender approved or declined it.

Funding intelligence for the SME

EeziFund can identify strengths, missing information and mandate-related blockers so the SME understands what may improve fundability rather than receiving only a yes or no.

Privacy and routing remain separate

EeziFund may use broader market and mandate intelligence to assess fit. Applicant information is only routed where the funding partner is participating, its mandate is confirmed and routing has been explicitly activated.

TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

EeziFund helps you navigate funding. It does not make the credit decision.

Free for applicants

EeziFund does not charge SMEs a registration, application or matching fee. The commercial model is intended to be funded through referral or partner economics where applicable.

Funders decide

Funding partners retain their own credit, pricing, legal, security and approval discretion. A potential match is not an approval or offer.

Your application stays controlled

A researched or potentially suitable funder does not automatically receive your information. Routing is a separate controlled step.
