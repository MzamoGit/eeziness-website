ABOUT EEZINESS

Technology should remove friction — not create more of it.

Eeziness builds practical, focused AI-enabled tools for real-world business and personal workflows. The aim is simple: take processes that are expensive, confusing, repetitive or administratively heavy and make them easier to understand and complete.

Eeziness was founded by a South African Chartered Accountant with deep experience across finance, private equity, governance, risk and strategy. That practical operating experience shapes the products: useful outputs, clear workflows and strong human oversight matter more than AI for its own sake.
