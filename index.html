THE SME READINESS GAP • BUILT FOR SOUTH AFRICAN SMEs

Good businesses can be held back by readiness gaps.

SMEs can have customers, opportunity and ambition — yet still struggle to access funding or scale because the business is not institutionally ready. Financial information may be late or unreliable; governance and compliance may be weak; systems and processes may be informal; strategy may not be executable; and investor documentation may be scattered.

Independent market validation: In a recent SAFM Market Update radio interview, Thabang Hleza, Head of Investments at Old Mutual’s Masisizane Fund, described the same investment-readiness gap — reinforcing the importance of financial management, governance, operational capability and growth planning. THE CONNECTED RESPONSE

One business. Connected solutions.

An SME rarely has one isolated problem. Outdated financials can block funding. A tender can expose compliance gaps. A strategic priority can reveal new risks and stakeholder dependencies. EeziNess starts with the business problem and connects the right enablers as the next need becomes clear.

CONNECTED BY DESIGN

One problem often exposes another.

The value of EeziNess is not only what each enabler does on its own. The enablers can hand work to one another when the underlying issue sits elsewhere — so the business strengthens as a whole rather than collecting disconnected software.

EeziCFO→EeziFund

EeziCFO spots a working-capital gap. EeziFund helps identify the funding route.

EeziFund→EeziFinance

A funding application is weakened by outdated financial information. EeziFinance helps get the books current before reassessment.

EeziBid→EeziComply

A tender exposes missing company or governance records. EeziComply helps close the compliance gap.

EeziStrategy→EeziRisk+EeziPulse

A strategic priority is set. EeziRisk and EeziPulse help manage what could derail it and the relationships that matter.

EeziDebt

EeziDebt has grown beyond debt organisation into a continuously updated personal financial-wellness enabler: understand the whole household picture, improve debt outcomes and see how affordability changes over time.

Build the full picture Upload debt statements, payslips, proof of other income and bank statements. EeziDebt extracts and organises the information into one view.
Living income & expenditure Detect recurring bank payments, add expenses that do not appear on the statement, review possible duplicates and maintain a live monthly household budget.
Debt strategy & optimisation Compare Avalanche, Snowball and cash-flow-relief approaches, settlement options and extra-payment scenarios, including potential time and interest savings.
Creditor & legal support Track arrears, settlement offers and legal notices, keep source evidence linked, and prepare practical creditor-action drafts where needed.
What you may qualify for See indicative affordability ranges for a home loan, vehicle finance and personal loan. These are guidance only — not an application, approval, quote or guarantee.
Ongoing guidance Monthly snapshots, position-change detection and Ask EeziDebt help users see whether their financial position is improving. The service is free; lender referrals require explicit user consent.
